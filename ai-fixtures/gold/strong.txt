In my final year I led a team of four students building a flood warning system for our town. Our first prototype kept giving false alarms because the water sensor readings were noisy. I split the problem into two parts: one teammate tested the hardware in a water tank, while I and another teammate wrote a filter that averages readings over ten minutes. After two weeks of testing the false alarms dropped from about twelve a day to one a week. What I learned is to break a vague problem into testable pieces and to check in with everyone every few days, so that problems appear early instead of at the deadline.
